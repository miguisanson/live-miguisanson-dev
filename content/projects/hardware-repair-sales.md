## Overview

This work involved repairing and selling gaming consoles and older hardware through customer-facing channels.

## Problem

Hardware issues often require careful diagnosis, clear communication, and practical replacement decisions.

## Solution

The work developed troubleshooting habits across component replacement, basic diagnostics, repair documentation, and customer coordination.

## Learnings

- Hardware troubleshooting rewards careful observation.
- Clear customer communication matters as much as technical diagnosis.
- Repair work builds patience with systems that fail in non-obvious ways.
