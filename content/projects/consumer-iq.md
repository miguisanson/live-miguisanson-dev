## Overview

Consumer IQ is a presentation-grade prototype for consumer intelligence, competitor analysis, operations monitoring, incident handling, and AI-assisted insight workflows.

## Problem

Business and operations teams need a single place to interpret customer signals, understand competitor movement, and keep dashboard/data reliability visible.

## Solution

The prototype uses deterministic mock data and local UI state to simulate dashboards, role-aware areas, quick insights, incident queues, self-healing flows, and human handoff records.

## Learnings

- Operational reliability should be visible in the product, not hidden behind the scenes.
- Mock AI flows are useful for validating workflow quality before connecting paid APIs.
- Role-focused navigation keeps a complex demo easier to understand.
